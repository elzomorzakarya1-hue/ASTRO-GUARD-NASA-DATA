Study design
Four-person Inspiration4 crew · urine biospecimens collected before flight and during post-flight recovery · L-92, L-44, L-3, R+1, R+45 and R+82 timepoint labels. Samples were frozen at −80 °C before assay processing. The listed sample set is 22 samples; do not assume a complete 4-person × 6-timepoint matrix.
Assay and value semantics
NULISAseq 200plex v1 Inflammation Panel · multiplex immunoassay · Alamar Biosciences. The study describes two technical replicates per sample. Reported NPQ values are log2 IPC-normalized counts; retain the source’s NPQ label and do not convert them to concentration or clinical units.
Correct use in ASTRO-GUARD
Suitable for a carefully scoped, retrospective exploratory analysis of assay-reported inflammatory protein patterns. Not suitable as continuous heart-rate, blood-pressure, oxygen-saturation or real-time crew-health telemetry. Technical replicates are not independent biological subjects. Do not impute missing crew/timepoint combinations or treat NPQ as a validated clinical threshold.
